A RHYTHM · GESTURE · DUNGEON CRAWLERDUNGEON OF
THE DOWNBEAT

Eight floors. Eight bosses. Eight tracks. Swipe your blade, draw your spells, and land every move on the beat.

Headphones or speakers on. Best with a mouse and keyboard.

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE

  • WS forward / back
  • AD strafe
  • QE turn (or arrow keys)
  • Esc pause, jukebox, beat offset
  • H toggle the gesture sheet

BLADE — DRAG THE MOUSE

  • Slash swipe left or right
  • Cleave swipe down, ×1.4
  • Thrust swipe up, crushes staggered foes
  • Guard draw a peak, shields 2 beats
  • Alternating moves adds +20% flow damage. Foes have weaknesses.

SPELLS — DRAW THE RUNE

  • Firebolt V · 3 MP · long range
  • Lightning Z · 5 MP · stuns
  • Mend circle · 4 MP · heals
  • Mana only comes back from on-beat hits and parries.

THE BEAT

  • Release your stroke on the beat. PERFECT = ×2, GOOD = ×1.3, off-beat = ×0.6.
  • Every on-beat action builds GROOVE, up to ×2 more. Max it for FEVER.
  • A missed attack drops a groove tier. An off-beat step resets groove to zero — turning is free. One action counts per beat.

DEFENCE

  • Enemies wind up for two beats and show an arrow. Swipe that direction as the ring closes to PARRY: no damage, a stagger and a riposte.
  • A purple peak means unblockable: Guard it. A perfect guard absorbs everything.

BOSSES & POWERUPS

  • Every floor has a boss. The stairs stay sealed until it falls.
  • Glowing gems last a set number of beats: Fury (double damage), Unbreakable (groove can't drop), Wide Beat (bigger timing windows), Mana Surge (free spells), Aegis (half damage taken).
